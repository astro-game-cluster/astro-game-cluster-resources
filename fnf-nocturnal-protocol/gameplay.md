# FNF Nocturnal Protocol Core Gameplay & Facts

## Overview
- **Game Title**: FNF Nocturnal Protocol (Friday Night Funkin' horror mod)
- **Franchise / Universe**: Slendytubbies (ZeoWorks) x Friday Night Funkin' (Myths of Tubbyland project)
- **Original Composer**: Sanco the Fox
- **Fan Recreation Director**: GammamonNiro (Niro)
- **V-Slice Porter**: danypr008
- **BPM**: 125 BPM
- **Track Duration**: Approximately 3 minutes 30 seconds
- **Protagonist**: The Guardian (White Tubby with flashlight & cow-pattern hat)
- **Opponent**: Mutated Tinky Winky

## Core Mechanics
1. **5 Musical Phases**:
   - 0:00 - 0:13: Isolated pure whistle intro (Twisted Nerve melody) in deep silence.
   - 0:13 - 0:53: Verse 1, low-frequency 808 bass enters, Tinky Winky's distorted scream joins.
   - 0:53 - 1:38: Chase 1, tempo accelerates, chromatic runs intensify as the Guardian flees.
   - 1:38 - 2:18: Bridge, whistle dominates, Custard Notes appear.
   - 2:18 - 3:00: Climax ("2:20 Drop"), violent mutation cutscene, peak note density, breakcore drums.
   - 3:00 - End: Outro, drums fade, dynamic ending triggered.

2. **Dual-Ending Custard Notes**:
   - 10 special green Custard Notes spawn during 1:38 - 2:18.
   - **Good Ending**: Collect all 10 Custard Notes (10/10) with >95% accuracy. Guardian escapes the ruins with research samples.
   - **Bad Ending**: Intentionally miss all 10 Custard Notes (0/10) while maintaining health on regular arrows. Guardian is captured and infected by Tinky Winky.

3. **Twisted Nerve Whistle Lineage**:
   - 1968: Composed by Bernard Herrmann for the psychological thriller film *Twisted Nerve*.
   - 2003: Daryl Hannah whistled it in Quentin Tarantino's *Kill Bill: Vol. 1*.
   - 2012: Adopted by ZeoWorks as the eerie BGM for the Slendytubbies "A Walk in the Park" trailer.
   - 2020: Adapted by Sanco the Fox into FNF rhythm battle.
