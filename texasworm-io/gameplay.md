## How to Play TexasWorm.io: Your Complete First-Time Guide
Welcome, aspiring worm cowboy! TexasWorm.io is an easy-to-learn game where you guide your worm to grow, dominate, and survive. This guide will walk you through the basics, and before you know it, you'll be the biggest worm in the Wild West!

### 1. Your Mission: The Objective
Your primary goal in TexasWorm.io is to grow your worm as large as possible by consuming food, outmaneuvering other players, and becoming the undisputed giant worm of the arena.

### 2. Taking Command: The Controls
**Disclaimer:** *These are the standard controls for this type of game on PC Browser with Keyboard/Mouse. The actual controls may be slightly different.*
| Action / Purpose | Key(s) / Gesture |
| :--- | :--- |
| Main Movement | Mouse Cursor (your worm follows) |
| Boost Speed | Left Mouse Button or Spacebar |
| Primary Action (e.g., Shoot, Jump, Select) | Not Applicable |

### 3. Reading the Battlefield: Your Screen (HUD)
*   **Your Worm's Length/Score:** Usually displayed prominently, this number indicates your current size and score. The higher it is, the more dominant you are!
*   **Leaderboard:** Often on the side or top of the screen, this shows the top players' scores. Keep an eye on it to see who you need to beat to reach the top!
*   **Food/Orbs:** These are the small, glowing dots scattered across the map. Consume them to grow!

### 4. The Rules of the World: Core Mechanics
*   **Growth and Consumption:** Move your worm over the scattered food dots to consume them. Each piece of food you eat makes your worm longer and increases your score. The longer you are, the more food you need to continue growing significantly.
*   **Boosting:** Use the boost key (Left Mouse Button or Spacebar) to temporarily increase your worm's speed. Be careful, though, as boosting consumes a small portion of your worm's length, effectively sacrificing size for speed. Use it strategically to escape danger or cut off opponents.
*   **Collision and Elimination:** The core rule of survival is to avoid hitting other worms' bodies with your head. If your worm's head touches another worm's body, you will be eliminated, and your worm's mass will turn into food for other players. Conversely, if another worm's head hits *your* body, *they* will be eliminated, and you can then consume their remains to grow even larger. Use your body to strategically block and trap smaller worms!

### 1. The Foundation: Three Golden Habits

To become a TexasWorm legend, you must first internalize these foundational habits. They are the bedrock upon which all elite-level play is built, ensuring consistency and setting you up for explosive scoring.

*   **Golden Habit 1: Constant Peripheral Awareness** - In `TexasWorm.io`, tunnel vision is a death sentence. This habit is about continuously scanning the entire screen, not just your immediate surroundings. You must track not only your worm's head but also its entire tail, the positions of all other worms, and the spawn locations of food pellets. This constant awareness allows for proactive movement, avoids accidental collisions, and identifies emerging opportunities for aggressive plays. It's critical because the chaotic nature of the game means threats and opportunities can materialize instantly from any direction.
*   **Golden Habit 2: Strategic Food Pathing** - Simply eating food isn't enough; you must eat with intent. This habit involves planning your movement path to consume multiple food pellets efficiently, often in a serpentine pattern that maximizes growth per unit of time. Avoid chasing single, isolated pellets unless absolutely necessary. Prioritize areas with dense food spawns or pellets that naturally align with your desired growth trajectory. This is critical for maximizing your size-to-time ratio, which directly translates to a larger presence and intimidating power on the map.
*   **Golden Habit 3: Predictive Aggression** - Good players react; great players anticipate. This habit is about using your peripheral awareness to predict the movements of other worms and initiating aggressive maneuvers *before* they pose a direct threat or when an opportunity to trap them arises. Look for worms heading towards bottlenecks, those extending their tails into vulnerable positions, or those who are clearly focused on a distant food source. This proactive aggression allows you to dictate engagements, secure kills, and claim prime territory, rather than merely responding to others.

### 2. Elite Tactics: Mastering the Scoring Engine

TexasWorm.io's scoring engine is fundamentally built around **Resource Efficiency and Risk Management**, with a hidden emphasis on **Strategic Elimination**. While growing large through food consumption is important, the true high scores come from efficiently converting other worms into mass, while simultaneously minimizing your own risk. These tactics exploit this core engine.

*   **Advanced Tactic: The "Coiled Trap" Maneuver**
    *   **Principle:** This tactic involves intentionally circling a smaller or similarly sized worm, gradually tightening the coil to force them into an inescapable situation, turning them into a prime food source. It's a calculated risk to yield massive growth.
    *   **Execution:** First, identify a target that is either smaller, distracted, or moving predictably. Position your worm to begin a wide circle around them, ensuring your head is always outside their potential escape path. Then, slowly and deliberately tighten the circle, always keeping your tail protected from their head. You must resist the urge to rush the squeeze; patience is key. Finally, as the space shrinks, the target will panic and inevitably collide with your body, yielding a significant mass bonus.
*   **Advanced Tactic: The "Border Control" Strategy**
    *   **Principle:** This strategy leverages the map's boundaries as a natural barrier, creating choke points and limiting enemy escape routes, thereby increasing the efficiency of your traps and reducing the surface area you need to defend. It's about territorial dominance through environmental exploitation.
    *   **Execution:** Initially, focus your growth and movement along the map's edges. As you gain size, establish a "patrol route" along a significant portion of the border. When you encounter smaller worms or those attempting to move along the border, use your body to cut off their escape towards the center, forcing them to either collide with the wall or with your body. The key is to control the narrowest parts of the map, turning the environment into your ally and herding opponents into lethal zones.

### 3. The Pro Secret: A Counter-Intuitive Edge

Most players think that constantly chasing the largest, most visible food pellets is the best way to play. They are wrong. The true secret to breaking the 500k score barrier is to do the opposite: **prioritize the elimination of medium-sized worms over pure food farming, even if it means sacrificing some immediate food intake**. Here's why this works: eliminating a medium-sized worm not only removes a competitor but instantly grants you a significant, concentrated burst of mass (their entire body) far more efficiently than grazing for hundreds of small pellets. This mass gain is immediate, consolidates your growth, and clears the field, allowing you to dominate subsequent food spawns and set up more traps with greater confidence. It's a high-risk, high-reward approach that, when executed correctly, creates an unstoppable snowball effect.
