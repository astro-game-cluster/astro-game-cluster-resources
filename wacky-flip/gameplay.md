## How to Play Wacky Flip: Your Complete First-Time Guide

Welcome to Wacky Flip! This skill-based simulator is all about timing, control, and style. Don't worry about the complex tricks yet; the game is designed to teach you the basics through simple, encouraging challenges right from the start. Follow this guide, and you'll be performing impressive flips and sticking perfect landings like a pro in no time.

### 1. Your Mission: The Objective

Your immediate goal is to successfully perform a required flip or trick (or sequence of tricks) and **land smoothly** on the designated platform without falling or tumbling. Each successful landing completes a challenge and unlocks the next, progressively more difficult challenge, moving you one step closer to becoming the ultimate backflip athlete.

### 3. Reading the Battlefield: Your Screen (HUD)

The screen provides critical feedback on your performance and progress. Watching these elements is key to mastering your timing and maximizing your score.

*   **Score Multiplier:** Typically displayed in the top-center, this number increases with consecutive successful flips and combo tricks. **Why it's important:** A higher multiplier dramatically boosts your final score for that challenge. Focus on combo-ing your tricks!
*   **Flip/Trick Indicator:** Often a small icon or text near your character, showing the type of skill (e.g., Pike, Scissor, Backflip) currently being executed. **Why it's important:** Use this to ensure you are executing the correct move and judging when to lock your body position for the landing.
*   **Target Landing Zone:** The platform you must land on, often highlighted or clearly distinct. **Why it's important:** You must land entirely within this zone to pass the challenge. Pay attention to its position and height relative to your jump-off point.
*   **Current Level/Challenge Number:** Located in a corner, showing your progress (e.g., The Court 1/10). **Why it's important:** Reminds you how close you are to unlocking the next set of environments and skills.

### 4. The Rules of the World: Core Mechanics

Wacky Flip is governed by three foundational rules: **The Jump, The Flip, and The Landing.** Master these, and you master the game.

*   **The Jump & Flip System:** **If** you press and hold the main action button (Spacebar), your character will begin the jump and initiate the rotational force for the flip. **Then**, you must **release** the button at the precise moment to lock the rotation and prepare for the landing. Holding too long results in over-rotation; releasing too early results in under-rotation.
*   **The Landing Requirement:** **If** your character lands anywhere outside the target platform, or if they land with an unstable stance (a tumble or a fall), the challenge fails. **Therefore**, you must use subtle, last-second adjustments (A/D or Arrow keys) just before hitting the platform to ensure your feet hit flat.
*   **Skill Progression & Scoring:** **If** you successfully complete challenges, you will unlock more complex skills (like the Knee Tuck or Pike). **Then**, performing these difficult skills, especially in sequence (combos), will greatly increase your score and score multiplier, allowing you to achieve the highest possible rank for that challenge.
