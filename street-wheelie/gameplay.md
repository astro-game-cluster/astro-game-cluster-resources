## How to Play Street Wheelie: Your Complete First-Time Guide

Welcome to Street Wheelie! This game is all about balance, control, and style. While mastering the perfect wheelie takes practice, the basic controls are simple and intuitive. Follow this guide, and you will be pulling off impressive stunts and earning high scores in no time. Get ready to hit the road and experience the thrill!

### 1. Your Mission: The Objective

Your primary goal is to **perform and maintain the longest possible wheelie** without crashing or letting your front wheel touch the ground. The game rewards delicate throttle control and precise balance, which translate directly into higher scores and unlockable rewards.

### 2. Taking Command: The Controls

**Crucial Input:** Given the physics-based nature of precise balance control, this game is highly likely designed for **PC Browser with Keyboard**.

**Disclaimer:** *These are the standard controls for this type of game on PC Browser with Keyboard. The actual controls may be slightly different.*

| Action / Purpose | Key(s) / Gesture |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Throttle / Accelerate** | **W** or **Up Arrow** |
| **Brake / Decelerate** | **S** or **Down Arrow** |
| **Lean Backward (Lift Front Wheel)** | **Spacebar** or **Down Arrow** (while moving) |
| **Balance Lean Left** | **A** or **Left Arrow** |
| **Balance Lean Right** | **D** or **Right Arrow** |
| **Change Camera View** | **C** Key |

### 3. Reading the Battlefield: Your Screen (HUD)

Understanding your Heads-Up Display (HUD) is essential for monitoring your performance and preventing a crash.

*   **Speedometer (MPH/KMH):** Usually centered on the screen, this shows your current speed. Maintaining a moderate, consistent speed is key to a smooth, long wheelie.
*   **Wheelie Distance/Time Counter:** This is your score tracker. It measures how long (in seconds) or how far (in meters) you have successfully maintained the wheelie. Watch this number climbâitâs a direct measure of your skill!
*   **Throttle/Balance Gauge:** Often a small visual indicator, this shows your current throttle input and your bike's degree of lean. Use this to fine-tune your balance; if the lean indicator is too high, you are about to flip backward!
*   **Best Score:** Typically in the corner, this displays your all-time high score. Aim to beat this number every time you ride!

### 4. The Rules of the World: Core Mechanics

Mastering the following 2-3 rules will allow you to transition from simply riding to expertly performing a wheelie. Control is everything.

*   **The Initiation Rule (Lifting Off):** To begin a wheelie, you must first accelerate (W/Up Arrow Key) to a moderate speed. Then, quickly tap and hold the **Lean Backward/Spacebar** key. The combination of speed and lean will lift the front wheel off the ground.
*   **The Balance Rule (Maintaining the Wheelie):** Once the wheel is up, you must balance the bike using the **Lean Left (A)** and **Lean Right (D)** keys. If the bike starts to fall to one side, tap the opposite direction to correct it. Delicate, tiny adjustments are much better than large, sudden movements.
*   **The Throttle Control Rule (Sustaining the Ride):** Your speed stabilizes the wheelie. If you feel the bike is falling backward, *reduce* the throttle (S/Down Arrow Key) slightly to drop the front end a bit. If the front wheel is dropping too fast, *increase* the throttle (W/Up Arrow Key) slightly to bring it back up. This constant, subtle interplay between throttle and side-to-side balance is the secret to a world-class wheelie.
