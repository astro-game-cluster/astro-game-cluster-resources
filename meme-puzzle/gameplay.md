### 3. Reading the Battlefield: Your Screen (HUD)

The interface is clean and designed not to distract you from the puzzle. Focus on these key elements to track your progress:

*   **Completion Progress Bar:** Usually located at the top of the screen, this visually tracks how many pieces you have correctly placed versus the total number of pieces. Watch it fill up as you get closer to solving the meme!
*   **Timer/Moves Counter (If applicable):** Often found in a top corner, this tracks either the time elapsed or the number of moves you have made. In timed modes, keeping this low is crucial for a high score.
*   **Hint Button:** Typically a small icon (like a lightbulb) in the corner. Using this will briefly highlight the correct location for a random piece, but often results in a score penalty, so use it only when truly stuck.
*   **Meme Preview:** In some modes, a faded, complete version of the meme might be visible in the background. This is your most important visual aid, showing you the target image you need to re-create.

### 4. The Rules of the World: Core Mechanics

Understanding these core mechanics will help you solve puzzles faster and more efficiently:

*   **Correct Placement Feedback:** When you drag a puzzle piece close to its correct location, it will "snap" into place, giving you satisfying visual and auditory feedback. If it doesn't snap, the piece is either in the wrong spot or needs to be rotated.
*   **Edge and Corner Strategy:** Always start by identifying the straight-edged pieces. These are the borders of the image. Solving the border first provides a fixed frame of reference, making it much easier to place the interior, more complicated pieces.
*   **Visual Pattern Matching:** The key to speed is not trial-and-error, but pattern matching. Before dragging a piece, look at the visual elements (colors, lines, text) on the piece and match them to the corresponding elements on the preview or already assembled section of the image.
*   **Progressive Difficulty:** As you advance, the game will introduce more complex puzzle cuts (e.g., irregular shapes, fewer straight edges) and increase the total number of pieces. Take your time, and remember that patience is the ultimate tool.
