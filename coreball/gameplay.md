## How to Play Coreball: Your Complete First-Time Guide
Welcome to Coreball! This guide will quickly get you up to speed on how to play this addictive agility game. Don't worry, it's easy to pick up, and you'll be challenging your reflexes and scoring big in no time!

### 1. Your Mission: The Objective
Your primary goal in Coreball is to precisely launch smaller balls into a rotating core without colliding with any other balls already attached to it. Successfully attaching balls will advance you through levels and increase your score, challenging your agility and timing.

### 2. Taking Command: The Controls
**Disclaimer:** *These are the standard controls for this type of game on PC Browser with Keyboard/Mouse. The actual controls may be slightly different.*
| Action / Purpose | Key(s) / Gesture |
| :--- | :--- |
| Launch Ball | Left Mouse Click or Spacebar |

### 3. Reading the Battlefield: Your Screen (HUD)
*   **Current Score:** Typically located at the top of the screen, this shows how many balls you have successfully attached to the core across all levels. Watch it grow as your precision improves!
*   **Balls Remaining:** Usually displayed near your launching point, this indicates how many balls you have left to attach in the current level. Run out before filling the core, and it's game over!
*   **Level Indicator:** Often found at the top or bottom, this simply tells you which level you are currently on. Each level presents a new challenge with a different core rotation and ball arrangement.

### 4. The Rules of the World: Core Mechanics
*   **Precision is Key:** You must launch your ball precisely into an empty space on the rotating core. If your launched ball hits another ball already attached to the core, the game ends!
*   **Rotation Changes:** Be aware that the core's rotation speed and direction can change with each level, demanding quick adaptation to new patterns. Observe the rotation before you launch!
*   **Strategic Launches:** Sometimes it's better to wait for the perfect opening rather than rushing. Patiently observe the gaps and anticipate the core's movement to ensure a successful attachment.
