## How to Play Words or Die: Your Complete First-Time Guide
Welcome to Words or Die! Get ready for a thrilling linguistic challenge. This guide will quickly get you up to speed, transforming you from a new recruit to a word-slinging veteran in no time. Let's dive in and master the art of word survival!

### 1. Your Mission: The Objective
Your primary goal in Words or Die is to survive as long as possible by forming words from given letters to build barriers and prevent the rising lava from engulfing you. The longer you last, the higher your score will be!

### 2. Taking Command: The Controls
**Disclaimer:** *These are the standard controls for this type of game on PC Browser with Keyboard/Mouse. The actual controls may be slightly different.*
| Action / Purpose | Key(s) / Gesture |
| :--- | :--- |
| Select Letters / Type Word | Keyboard | 
| Submit Word | Enter Key |
| Clear Current Word | Esc or Backspace Key |

### 3. Reading the Battlefield: Your Screen (HUD)
*   **Given Letters:** Usually displayed prominently, these are the letters you must use to form words. Keep a close eye on them to spot potential words quickly.
*   **Lava Level Indicator:** This meter or visual cue shows how high the lava has risen. If it reaches the top, it's game over! Form words quickly to push it back down.
*   **Score/Time Survived:** Typically in the top-right, this tracks your current score or how long you've managed to survive. Watch it climb as you outwit the lava!
*   **Word Input Area:** This is where you'll see the word you are currently forming. Make sure your words are valid before submitting!

### 4. The Rules of the World: Core Mechanics
*   **Word Formation:** You must connect adjacent letters (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to form valid words. Longer and more complex words will be more effective.
*   **Building Blockers:** Each valid word you submit creates a blocker or pushes down the lava level. The stronger the word, the more impact it has on the lava.
*   **Rising Lava:** The lava continuously rises, threatening to consume your play area. Your only defense is to rapidly form words to build barriers and keep the lava at bay. Failure to do so will end your game.

### 1. The Foundation: Three Golden Habits

Before we delve into advanced maneuvers, internalize these three golden habits. They are the bedrock upon which every high score is built. Neglect them at your peril.

*   **Golden Habit 1: Proactive Vocabulary Scanning** - In Words or Die, reactive word-finding is a death sentence. This habit is about constantly scanning the available letters, not just for immediate words, but for potential prefixes, suffixes, and letter clusters that can form longer, higher-scoring words. The "why" is simple: longer words mean more points and more blockers. Train your eyes to see patterns and possibilities before the timer pressure truly mounts.
*   **Golden Habit 2: Dynamic Blocker Placement** - Your blockers are your lifelines. This isn't just about creating a wall; it's about strategic defense. This habit demands you consider not only the immediate threat of the lava but also how your placed blockers will influence future letter drops and potential word formations. The "why" is twofold: efficient blocker placement buys you critical time and can funnel letters into more advantageous positions for future plays, maximizing your resource efficiency.
*   **Golden Habit 3: The "Hot Streak" Mindset** - Words or Die rewards momentum. This habit is about maintaining a continuous flow of word creation. Avoid pauses, hesitations, and indecision. The "why" is directly tied to the combo system; a consistent stream of words keeps your multiplier active, transforming good plays into phenomenal scores. Think of it as a rhythmic dance with the keyboard, where every beat is a new word.

### 2. Elite Tactics: Mastering the Scoring Engine

With the fundamentals ingrained, let's elevate your gameplay with tactics designed to exploit the game's scoring engine.

*   **Advanced Tactic: The "Lava Skim" Combo**
    *   **Principle:** This tactic is about intentionally allowing the lava to rise to a critical level, creating a high-pressure scenario that, when expertly navigated, triggers a massive score multiplier from rapid, successive word creations as you "skim" the danger zone.
    *   **Execution:** First, you need to identify a moment where you have a strong mental inventory of 3-4 potential words. Then, you must resist the urge to place blockers immediately, instead focusing on forming words and allowing the lava to creep upwards. Finally, when the lava is perilously close to your current word-line, you activate a rapid-fire sequence of pre-identified words, ideally using longer words to generate significant blockers and push the lava back drastically, resulting in a cascading combo bonus.
*   **Advanced Tactic: The "Prefix Funnel" Strategy**
    *   **Principle:** This involves deliberately leaving common prefixes (e.g., "RE-", "UN-", "DE-") or suffixes exposed on the board, using them as anchors to quickly build multiple longer words as new letters drop, creating a "funnel" for high-scoring combinations.
    *   **Execution:** Initially, prioritize creating words that leave behind these valuable prefix/suffix letter combinations. As new letters fall, constantly scan for opportunities to attach them to your funnels. The key is to have a mental library of words that start or end with these common elements. This minimizes search time and accelerates word formation, directly feeding into your speed and combo efficiency.

### 3. The Pro Secret: A Counter-Intuitive Edge

Most players think that **always making the longest possible word** is the best way to play. They are wrong. The true secret to breaking the 500k score barrier is to do the opposite: **strategically sacrifice a longer word for two medium-length words that clear more immediate threats or set up future combos.** Here's why this works: While a single long word gives a good score, two medium words (especially if they are quick to form and clear crucial space) often result in a higher *effective score per second* and maintain your combo multiplier more reliably. Furthermore, clearing more letters faster means better board control and more opportunities for subsequent high-scoring plays, ultimately leading to a far greater overall score than painstakingly hunting for that one perfect, massive word.
