The core of Tung Sahur Clicker's high-score potential lies in its **Speed & Combos** engine. Every mechanic, every reward, every challenge funnels back to how efficiently you can maintain maximum tapping speed and chain together consecutive "wakes." Our tactics are designed to exploit this.
