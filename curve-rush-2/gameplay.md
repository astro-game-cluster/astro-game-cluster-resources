### 3. Reading the Battlefield: Your Screen (HUD)

The screen provides vital information for managing your run and tracking your success. Keep an eye on these critical elements to maximize your distance.

*   **Distance/Score Counter:** Typically located in the top corner, this displays your current score, which is based on the distance youâve traveled and the number of successful jumps over the horizon line. Watch this number climb as you master the momentum!
*   **Coin Counter:** Usually shown near the score, this tracks the number of Golden Coins you have collected during the current run (or total accumulated coins). Collect these to unlock new balls and environments.
*   **Momentum/Speed:** While not a dedicated meter, pay attention to the visual speed of the ball. Max speed means you've successfully preserved your flow, while slowing down indicates you need to quickly find a downhill slope or execute a perfect jump.
*   **Horizon Line:** The white line separating the ground from the sky is your scoring target. Every time your ball soars *above* this line after a successful launch, you earn bonus points.

### 4. The Rules of the World: Core Mechanics

Curve Rush 2 is a game of physics and timing. Understand these core rules to keep your run going and your score soaring.

*   **Momentum Management:** If you **hold** the control button (Mouse or Spacebar) while going downhill, you will gain speed. If you **release** on an incline or the crest of a hill, that built-up speed is converted into a powerful jump, launching the ball forward and upward.
*   **The Perfect Landing:** If you **land smoothly** after a jump (matching the angle of the slope you land on), you will preserve your speed and maintain your momentum chain. However, if you **land poorly** (hitting a steep incline or flat ground at a high velocity), your run will instantly end, so timing is critical.
*   **Aero Scoring:** You receive score bonuses and points when your ball **soars above the white horizon line**. The higher and longer you fly, the greater the reward, encouraging you to seek out and master massive jumps.
*   **Coin Collection:** You must **touch** the scattered golden coins to collect them. These coins are the game's currency and are essential for unlocking new cosmetic features and challenges in the main menu.
