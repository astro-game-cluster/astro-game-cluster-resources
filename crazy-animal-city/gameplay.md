### 3. Reading the Battlefield: Your Screen (HUD)

The game provides a few critical on-screen elements to help you manage your rampage and stay alive. Watch these closely to keep your mayhem going!

*   **Rampage/Score Counter:** Typically located in the top-right corner, this tracks your overall destruction score. This score determines your high-score ranking, so watch it climb as you smash more objects.
*   **Health Bar:** Usually displayed near your animal, this shows how much damage you can take before being captured. If it drops to zero, the SWAT team successfully ends your run. Keep an eye on it after any run-in with the police!
*   **Coin Total:** This is your currency, tracked somewhere on the screen (often top-left). Every time you smash a crate or box, this number increases. You need these coins to upgrade and purchase new, stronger animals.
*   **SWAT Alert Level:** A visual indicator, sometimes a siren icon or a meter, that shows how actively the SWAT team is pursuing you. The higher the level, the more aggressive and numerous the agents will be. Keep moving to lose the heat!

### 4. The Rules of the World: Core Mechanics

Crazy Animal City has simple rules: cause destruction, get paid, and avoid the police. Mastering these mechanics is key to a long and successful rampage.

*   **The Mayhem-to-Coin Rule:** If you see a destructible object (like cars, crates, or environmental props), smash it! Destroying objects increases your Rampage Score and, more importantly, **smashing crates/boxes releases gold coins** which you need for upgrades.
*   **The Upgrade Cycle:** Coins are the only way to get better. If you collect enough coins, you can unlock more powerful animals (like the Lion, Gorilla, or T-Rex). **Stronger animals are more durable and can cause more destruction**âmaking it easier to survive longer against the SWAT team.
*   **The SWAT Avoidance Protocol:** The SWAT agents will always chase you and try to capture you. **If a SWAT agent attacks you, your Health Bar will decrease.** Do not stand still when they are near. Your goal is to keep constantly moving and use the environment (buildings, corners) to break their line of sight and escape.
*   **Environment Exploration:** **If you explore the map thoroughly, you may find hidden villages and treasures.** These secret locations often contain a large amount of coins or may provide temporary cover from the SWAT pursuit, so make exploration a part of your rampage strategy.
