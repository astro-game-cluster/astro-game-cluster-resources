### 2. Elite Tactics: Mastering the Scoring Engine

The core scoring engine of Bombardino Crocodilo Clicker is driven by **Resource Efficiency and Exponential Scaling**. High scores aren't about random clicking; they are about intelligently funneling resources (coins) into the most impactful upgrades that provide the highest return on investment (ROI) for both click power and automated generation, all while strategically utilizing the Ascension mechanic for compounding multipliers.

*   **Advanced Tactic: The "Early Auto-Gen Rush"**
    *   **Principle:** This tactic prioritizes securing early automated coin generation over raw click power, even if it means slightly slower initial manual clicking. The goal is to establish a passive income stream as quickly as possible, allowing for continuous coin accumulation even when you're not actively clicking, thus freeing up mental bandwidth for upgrade planning.
    *   **Execution:** For the first few thousand coins, resist the urge to solely dump into "Click Power." Instead, identify the cheapest auto-generation upgrades and pour your initial earnings into them. Once you have a respectable passive income established (enough to buy a low-tier auto-gen every few seconds passively), then you begin to balance with click power. This creates a powerful synergy where your clicks boost your immediate earnings, which in turn fund more auto-gen, which then generates more passive income for even more upgrades.
*   **Advanced Tactic: The "Ascension Stagger"**
    *   **Principle:** This involves deliberately staggering your Ascensions not just at max possible multiplier, but at specific, pre-determined breakpoints in your new run. The idea is to hit certain upgrade thresholds or skin unlocks faster in subsequent runs by exploiting the compounded Ascension Multiplier, rather than always pushing for the absolute maximum before resetting.
    *   **Execution:** After your first Ascension, you'll have a multiplier. Instead of grinding endlessly in the second run, monitor your progress. If you notice that with your new multiplier, you can achieve certain critical upgrade combinations or unlock a set of skins significantly faster by Ascending slightly earlier than the absolute maximum, do it. This creates a "snowball" effect, where each staggered Ascension propels you through the early game of the next run at an accelerated pace, leading to faster access to higher-tier upgrades and ultimately, a higher peak score.

### 3. The Pro Secret: A Counter-Intuitive Edge

Most players think that **endlessly grinding a single run for the highest possible coin count before Ascending** is the best way to play. They are wrong. The true secret to breaking the 500k score barrier is to do the opposite: **embrace frequent, strategically timed Ascensions even if you could push a little further in your current run.** Here's why this works: The "Ascension Multiplier" is the single most powerful scaling factor in the game. By Ascending more frequently, you accumulate this multiplier faster, causing each subsequent run to generate coins at an exponentially higher rate from the very beginning. While a single, long run might net you more coins *in that run*, the cumulative effect of many shorter, multiplier-enhanced runs will dramatically outpace it, unlocking skins and upgrades at a speed unimaginable to those who "play it safe."
